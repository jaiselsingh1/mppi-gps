usage: teacher_proposal.py [-h] --run RUN --out OUT
                           [--mode {preparation,round}]

Independent bounded readers and arithmetic for the H5.0 evidence stream. No
worker, planner, policy, simulator or production evidence decoder is imported.
Torch is imported only by the explicit saved-prediction verifier. These
helpers do not launch fitting or native replay. A structural stream check
alone is not the complete scientific round audit or a successful-loop
certificate.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --run RUN
  --out OUT
  --mode {preparation,round}
