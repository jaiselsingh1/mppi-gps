All six recorded teachers walked. Width 256 reduced final training MSE by approximately 47–49% in each pair, while validation MSE increased approximately 22–25%. All six final policies had 0/16 primary walking and survival; width 256 had higher complete cost and fewer executed controls in every pair. Reconstruction screen F passed, validation guard G and the task screen failed. Confirmation was correctly not triggered. These results show better fitted reconstruction with worse held-trajectory transfer under this intervention; they do not establish a general capacity limit or test adaptive coupling.
