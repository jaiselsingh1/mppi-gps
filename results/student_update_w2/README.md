Saved validation MSE was approximately 26% lower at 2k despite worse training reconstruction. **All six endpoints still had 0/16 walking and 0/16 full survival on each panel.** Walking-gain and competence criteria failed. This is confirmed partial control retention under this procedure, not competent walking, adaptive stopping or a closed MPPI–policy loop. Read the [causal verdict](../../docs/student_update_w2_results.md).

Retention and complete cost are related: reduced missing-control tail charges contributed −575.31/−615.21 cost on primary/confirmation, compared with total complete-cost changes of −385.88/−479.14. The [cost decomposition](accounting/engineering_accounting.json) prevents treating those two passing screens as independent evidence of improved gait.
