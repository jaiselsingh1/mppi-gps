# Portable research results

These small evidence capsules make the completed Walker studies readable from a clean clone. They contain exact original configurations, results, manifests, fitting curves, figures and audit evidence. Their `capsule.json` files preserve source paths, sizes and SHA-256 hashes. Bulk arrays, ledgers, checkpoints, datasets and videos remain outside Git.

| Study | Capsule | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| v3.1 fixed guided-data comparison | [guided_data_v3_1](guided_data_v3_1/README.md) | No walking student; guided lost independent survival benefit |
| L1 fresh clipped/full inputs | [student_learnability_l1](student_learnability_l1/README.md) | Both final students 0/16 walking and survival |
| H4.1 actual weight backoff | [adaptive_coupling_h4_1](adaptive_coupling_h4_1/README.md) | All six teachers walked; all matched children regressed |
| K1 Kendall action-compatibility diagnostic | [action_compatibility_k1](action_compatibility_k1/README.md) | Independent SAC reference qualified 16/16 +16/16; cloning reconstructed MPPI better but did not walk |
| W1 paired tanh-width contrast | [student_width_w1](student_width_w1/README.md) | Wider policies fit training data better, worsened validation, and all six final policies walked 0/16 |
| W2 fixed 2k/10k update budgets | [student_update_w2](student_update_w2/README.md) | Confirmed retention and cost benefits at 2k; all six endpoints still walked 0/16 on both panels |
| D1/D2 support and action consequences | [transfer_diagnosis_d1d2](transfer_diagnosis_d1d2/README.md) | All 20 replays matched exactly; support and fixed-suffix action effects do not yet explain failed transfer |

H4.1, K1, W1 and W2 full raw evidence are local-only. A capsule is not a replacement for its full frozen run and must not be used as a worker output directory.
