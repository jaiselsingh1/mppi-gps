usage: train_mppi_gps.py [-h] {prepare,fit,evaluate} ...

Matched source-corpus learning on CUDA, followed by native CPU evaluation. The
two create-only phases use separate interpreters and durable meters. They
share one sealed recipe, cumulative time/storage/work ceilings and immutable
CPU checkpoints. No planner, critic, relabelling or outcome selection occurs.

positional arguments:
  {prepare,fit,evaluate}

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
