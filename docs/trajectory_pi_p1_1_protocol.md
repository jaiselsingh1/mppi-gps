## Why the input version changes

R0.1 executed the original teacher slot0 controls on their origin macOS ARM
runtime and matched the original source trajectory byte exactly. Its independent
saved-evidence audit passed. Together with the preserved divergent Linux trace,
this supports a practical platform/runtime replayability distinction; it does
not identify a unique native arithmetic operation or prove all native defaults
identical. No PI optimization or learning occurred in R0.1.

The next gate is the separately frozen
[worker-native revalidation](trajectory_pi_native_baselines_protocol.md). It
captures and validates the same two original H4 control sequences on the Linux
worker. Their complete original initial contexts and float64 controls remain
byte-identical. The resulting Linux states, warmstarts, integration contexts,
observations and native costs are separately named, qualified and hash-bound;
they never overwrite the original macOS recordings.

The two worker-native capture costs are the immutable initial cost references
for the existing1.2-times-initial teacher admission and1.1-times-initial late

prevents silently mixing a native execution with a cost from another platform.
Both paths must still walk; neither may be selected, replaced or optimized
during validation.

## Frozen outcomes and interpretation

Apply every original confirmation criterion unchanged. The **same at least two**
paired streams must jointly show20% early-to-late teacher-gap contraction and a
late gap no more than.9 of alpha0, corresponding qualified contraction at both
student anchors, competent raw teachers and confirmed walking gains over both
alpha0 children and frozen incoming parents. All streams retain the original
walking/full-survival nonregression requirements. Missing or nonwalking query
coverage keeps that gap unresolved; a smaller gap from deteriorating teachers
cannot pass. Individual competence still requires15/16 walking on both panels.

A partial walking benefit, gap-only result or longer retention remains distinct
from confirming the finite loop hypothesis. Preserve all failures and declined
teacher proposals, all curves and accounting. Independently audit the saved
bindings, native comparisons, continuation, measurements and criteria. Publish
P1.1 separately from P1.0 and R0.1, then recommend one next discriminating step.

validation is added, and no broader project-success claim follows.
