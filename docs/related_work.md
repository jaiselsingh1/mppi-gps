# Claim-focused literature audit

Audit date: 2026-10-23. Scope: the proposed MPPI-GPS paper, inspired by Contact-Invariant Optimization (CIO) and Mordatch–Todorov (2014). These are not the project author's prior publications. This review began with primary papers and their references, before consulting project theory, objectives, or narrative experiment logs. Project-specific claim candidates below were supplied subsequently by the independent code/artifact audit.

**Verdict:** iterative planner-to-policy learning, policy-guided sampling, a KL-constrained exponential E-step, and cheap reactive deployment all have substantial prior art. VAMPS is the closest recent conceptual competitor. A narrower contribution about *verified finite-sample teacher updates under a declared compute budget* remains a research hypothesis; it is not established by the literature or current experimental record. Covariance scheduling also requires comparison to CoVO-MPC. The requested audit is **not complete**: no VAMPS implementation was located, the three recent OpenReview discussions could not be accessed, and no closest-competitor training run has been reproduced. A published PO-MPC statistic has been reproduced from official released run data and official analysis code; that is a separate, narrower accomplishment.

“Real environment steps” are physical interactions only when a physical robot is used. Training in MuJoCo must be called simulated environment interaction. One simulator trajectory is not one simulator step; one environment step may contain several physics substeps. Latent-model evaluations are a separate resource from full physics calls. The proposal should show both interaction-limited and compute-limited comparisons rather than hide planner work inside a sample-efficiency claim.

Include primary full texts and appendices for the named seeds and six roadmap items; distinguish papers, code, raw released results, and reviews. Abstracts were used only for discovery. ## What the six newer papers change about the proposal

**VAMPS is the closest conceptual threat.** Its iterative proprioceptive setting already uses the current policy to initialize MPPI, learns from refreshed optimized trajectories, adds terminal-value learning, and deploys a reactive policy. Its real-robot visual mode instead trains ACT once from a fixed planner-generated dataset; do not conflate those modes. BC and a mild penalty perform similarly while ADMM performs worse in its ablation; this directly challenges a presumption that tighter teacher/student coupling must help. Table I selects each method's best checkpoint, so it is not a precedent for a once-only final holdout. A fresh VAMPS replication is unavailable here because no author-linked implementation was located in the paper, author/arXiv links, or targeted code searches. This is an access/reproducibility gap, not evidence that the method fails. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.05331v1).

**SGPS differs through simulator derivatives and reference structure.** Its target-refinement acceptance test is a score in a nominal short segment, not a certificate about the subsequently trained policy's return. The visual formulation stops gradients through rendering but still needs differentiable dynamics/rewards for FoPG. Its nominal references and action targets must be counted as supervision. Comparisons that advertise a derivative-free training method should retain this distinction. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.20575v1).

**ProxPI makes prior mismatch a mandatory stress test.** Reinitializing around an incorrect prior can erase planner corrections; a larger rollout count need not repair this structural problem. Its local quadratic guarantees assume fixed landscape/prior/covariance and do not certify nonlinear contact learning. Particularly relevant: its reaching interpolation experiment fails across 50 seeds at prior mismatch where ESS/K is still about .26–.29. A healthy empirical ESS is not proof of finding task-relevant modes. [Full paper and Appendix C](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.00941v1).

**BMPC makes actor distillation a strong baseline.** It compares actor-only and planner execution, uses reanalysis, and changes the value-learning target. Matching its actor loss alone does not reproduce the complete method. Its interaction advantage also does not uniformly imply faster wall-clock: Walker time-to-threshold is slightly worse than TD-MPC2 in the published table. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.18871v2).

**PO-MPC separates two critics for a reason.** A KL-regularized policy objective changes the appropriate value target. Its λ→∞ identification is with a Q-based BMPC variant, not the full V-based BMPC algorithm; λ=0 recovers the relevant TD-MPC2-style objective. Appendix E's exact improvement arguments require the specified bounded/support conditions and fixed-prior reasoning, not arbitrary simultaneous neural fitting. [Full paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04280v2).

**TD-M(PC)² targets critic support mismatch.** It addresses distribution shift between planner-collected data and actor actions queried by TD targets. The final paper includes a behavior-mixture data ablation and more tasks than the early preprint. Its theoretical upper bounds do not alone prove a measured gap must grow, and the empirical overestimation results should be stated separately. [Final full paper](https://darthutopian.github.io/tdmpc_square/static/pdfs/paper_l4dc2026.pdf).

Two additional papers sharpen the scope. **CoVO-MPC already derives covariance design from quadratic MPPI.** Its population limit and fixed-determinant Hessian shaping differ from finite-label covariance annealing during student fitting; §7 explicitly leaves finite-sample and receding-horizon analysis to future work. Count Hessian computation or offline precomputation in C3 comparisons. Do not import its unrestricted Euclidean contraction assertion: for positive-definite noncommuting D and Σ, the update matrix can have norm above one. An independent [counterexample](audit_evidence/literature/covo_norm_counterexample.json) gives 1.11815, while the quadratic-cost metric still contracts. This qualification does not refute its commuting covariance design. [Final paper, Theorem1 and §7](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v242/yi24b/yi24b.pdf); [proof, Appendix A.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.07369v1).

**PL-MPC further narrows novelty around the planning–learning loop.** It combines realized-episode-return-weighted imitation, hybrid replay/model TD targets, and an uncertainty penalty for terminal scoring. Its results are explicitly task dependent: eight of thirteen HB means improve over TD-M(PC)², five worsen, and removing multistep TD improves hurdle. Distillation warmup helps some tasks and harms another. Hardware results use one selected training run per method, not independent training-seed replication; overlapping success intervals and unequal 2M/3M benchmark budgets limit strong comparative conclusions. These are useful ablations if the proposal adds critic learning, not evidence that a new global trust guarantee exists. [Full paper, §§IV–VI](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.39751v1).

## Relationship to the CIO motivation

The durable conceptual goal is to turn expensive task-specific optimization into a reusable feedback controller and to let the teacher adapt to what the student can learn. That is explicit in Mordatch–Todorov (2014), including quantitative comparisons to one-shot regression and feedback-gain supervision. The proposed project can advance that goal without claiming to originate it. Their Walker uses PD position targets and very small root actuation; it is not Gymnasium Walker2d torque control. [RSS paper, §§IV–V](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss10/p52.pdf).

The independent [2015 follow-up crosscheck](audit_evidence/mordatch2015_review.md) covers the entire paper and all five official reviews plus rebuttal. Its trajectory/network **proximal penalties** are not policy-KL constraints; its fixed loss weights do not establish competence-driven physical relaxation. Its deployed controller still solves a one-step optimization, so reactive-network timing alone would undercount execution cost. The reviewers' demands for controlled comparisons, data diversity and runtime detail apply directly here. [Paper](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/2612aa892d962d6f8056b195ca6e550d-Paper.pdf), [reviews and rebuttal](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/2612aa892d962d6f8056b195ca6e550d-Reviews.html).

CIO itself is not simply “a derivative-free way around contact.” The 2012 method introduces continuous contact-activation variables, allows contact helper forces during continuation, uses inverse dynamics and a simplified mass-at-torso model, and predefines potential contact patches and phase count. MPPI on unmodified MuJoCo forward dynamics changes both the optimization variables and the relaxation. Sampling handles nonsmooth costs without derivatives, but it does not inherit CIO's long-horizon contact-discovery evidence. A claim about recovering complex behavior from high-level task costs needs difficult contact-switching tasks and successful teacher discovery, not only tracking on Walker. [CIO full paper, §§3–6 and Appendix A](https://www.roboti.us/lab/papers/MordatchSIGGRAPH12.pdf).

The path-integral replacement has direct prior art too: PI²-GPS already adapts local action means and covariances through exponential weights, solves a KL-temperature dual, and supervises a global policy from newly sampled instances. Its physical examples depend on demonstrations and fixed local feedback gains. A simulator-resampled, receding-horizon teacher can differ meaningfully, but “PI²/MPPI plus GPS” is not an adequate novelty claim. [PI²-GPS, §§III–IV](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.00529).

## Threat note and required baselines

**Closest threat: VAMPS.** The overlapping story is already a complete algorithmic chain: simulator-based MPPI improvement, current-policy warm start, refreshed supervised data, optional coupling penalty/ADMM, learned terminal value, and reactive deployment. The proposal cannot credibly claim any of these alone as its main novelty. A defensible contribution would need a new, precisely specified budget/covariance mechanism, a proof restricted to its actual assumptions, and a measured advantage over a VAMPS-like iterative baseline at matched work. VAMPS's BC≈penalty result makes an unconstrained iterative-cloning control essential; its PPO episode-length ablation makes an under-tuned short-episode PPO comparison particularly vulnerable. The absence of released code prevents the strongest implementation-level comparison today.

Reviewers should reasonably demand these controls, scoped to the claims retained:

1. **C1:** raw MPPI at multiple K/H/iteration settings, policy-only inference, one-shot frozen BC, iterative DAgger/BC, and a VAMPS-like policy-warm-start teacher. Hold architecture, training states and labels constant when isolating coupling. Show student and teacher task outcomes separately.
2. **C2:** fixed-temperature and fixed-ESS teacher updates, a constrained PI²-GPS-style dual, no-coupling/no-relabel ablations, and prior-mismatch controls (vanilla nominal-centered MPPI, policy recentering, mixed proposals, ProxPI). Report actual achieved KL, ESS, importance corrections, rejection counts, covariance spectra and task return; ESS is a diagnostic.
3. **Any sample-efficiency claim:** SAC and properly tuned PPO on exactly the same environment/reward/termination/action interface, plus TD-MPC2 and at least BMPC/PO-MPC/TD-M(PC)² on a learned-model track. Exact-model oracle access must be declared; those competitors should not be charged for model learning while oracle physics is free for MPPI-GPS. Include equal-transition and equal-total-compute views.
4. **C3:** fixed covariance, empirical weighted-covariance adaptation (already in PI²-GPS), covariance annealing matched in total work, and [CoVO-MPC](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v242/yi24b/yi24b.pdf). Use independent noise/label replicates and an analytic LQR optimum. Separate covariance orientation from magnitude and temperature. CoVO-MPC's population analysis does not settle the finite-sample question; its Hessian access and preprocessing must be charged, and its implementation still needs inspection before a faithful empirical comparison.
5. **If claiming advantages over differentiable trajectory optimization:** MDGPS/iLQR and, where compatible, the SGPS/FoPG family. Count derivative evaluations and compilation. A contact task requiring demonstrations for one method but not another needs both initialized and uninitialized comparison settings.

Baseline tuning must use development resets only and a declared tuning budget. Keep one final checkpoint rule, one fresh reset set, and paired task starts across algorithms; retain training-seed independence. These design requirements follow from the claim, not from an assumption that a single benchmark score establishes generality.
