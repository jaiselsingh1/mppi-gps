Independent saved-only review of Acrobot's locked upright and basin joins.
This reviewer imports no simulator, planner or worker mathematics. It derives
the local native-map Jacobian, Riccati checks, deployed torques,
height/capture outcomes and adaptive MPPI weights from recorded arrays. Finite
basin probes remain empirical evidence, not a nonlinear invariant-set
certificate.
