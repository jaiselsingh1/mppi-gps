# Independent causal review of J1.0 development closure

The audited run closed at its declared development gate. It completed 32 matched learning cases in eight source groups, and never acquired or fitted the protected block. The primary MPPI motion standard deviation was 0.038493, below the frozen 0.1 threshold, and all MPPI standalone walking labels were failures. The five-score predictive objective remains **unidentified**, rather than a protected result showing that no score beat MSE. Scientific auditing passed; the similarity objective did not pass.

Both sources supplied eight walking teachers. MPPI-trained children walked on 0/256 standalone resets, with 2/256 full survivals; reference-trained children walked on 35/256, with 38/256 full survivals. Complete mean costs, including absorbing fall tails, were 4,754.40 and 3,725.95 respectively. These are 16 children per source nested within eight source groups, not 256 independent learning replications. The reference had more walking successes in eight paired learner contrasts and tied the other eight. Survival improved in eight contrasts, regressed in one and tied seven. Its mean cost was lower in 14 of the 16 contrasts. These source contrasts share incoming tensors, optimizer/RNG packages, minibatch tapes, data sizes, updates and reset panels, but change the entire source state/action distribution and trajectory structure together.

Reference walking came from eight children in six groups. The two best children, `development_00_s1_reference` and `development_04_s1_reference`, each walked on only 6/16 resets. No child approached the project's 15/16-on-two-panels competence requirement. This shows that the fixed width128 learner can occasionally learn walking in 2,000 updates; it does not establish a reliable baseline or prove that capacity/optimization suffices for the MPPI target family.

No child passed binary motion reproduction. Of 32 same-start executions, 28 fell before the full window. The sole full-length MPPI child did not walk and failed joint-angle, forward-velocity and final-progress thresholds. Three reference children walked from their teacher starts; all three missed the joint-angle threshold, and one also missed final progress. The two others satisfied velocity, height and final progress. Thus a failed reference-path reproduction is not automatically failed walking, and longer MPPI survival remains retention rather than locomotion. Missing post-fall progress was never converted into a full-window error.

The probe established a real distinction between planner memory, innovations and the tested hidden coordinates. Across four physical contexts from two source trajectories, carried-versus-zero nominal first-action MSE averaged 0.322, 0.391, 0.318 and 0.405. Private innovation changes also affected actions. Time/root-x/warmstart perturbations had zero or numerical-roundoff effects at the two tested contexts; this is a local result, not invariance over Walker. There were 208 decisions, not 208 independent task replications. All four local perturbation designs had rank17 and no observed contact-regime changes. At context0, both empirical gain matrices and held errors were exactly zero. At context1, held action RMSE was 0.0712 and 0.1268 for the two innovation streams. Design rank does not establish a nonzero controller gain, local linearity, optimality or harmless residual noise.

Teacher source matters under this update, while its causal explanation remains unresolved. The more successful source had slightly higher mean pre-update action MSE (0.306 versus 0.295), much higher smoothed-action MSE (0.279 versus 0.179), and lower state-explained proxy C (0.326 versus 0.606). These are development source summaries, not a protected win for C. The competent MPPI teachers also had lower task costs than the reference teachers, so task cost alone did not rank transfer quality here. Planner-memory/randomness effects do not show that smoothing, averaging, removing memory or a transport objective would improve learning.

The recommended next version is **J1.1-B, a development-only incoming-package/outcome-variation pilot**, specified in `next_proposal.md`. It tests whether including the student's developing behavior produces identifiable MPPI learning outcomes while separating learning gains from retained behavior with paired no-update execution controls. It retains all 16 reference children, including failures, and their original fresh parents without selecting favorable endpoints. This is benchmark repair, not a coupling mechanism. The existing probe remains useful evidence, but does not yet justify an intervention that erases consequential teacher variation. A new protected comparison would require a separate protocol and wholly separate pretraining/source/learner lineages after the pilot gates pass; nothing launches automatically.

The exact development-table bindings, case-level counts, paired source differences, reproduction failure components, probe summaries and worker/audit costs are in `review.json`. This review used saved audited metadata only and did not repeat inference, physics, fitting, prediction-model training or bootstrap comparisons.
