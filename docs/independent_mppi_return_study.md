# Independent MPPI experience and long-return policy learning

**Research draft, not a launch protocol.** On 2026-10-24 the user first
questioned adding SAC/a critic because the original goal was an alternative to
standard policy-gradient methods, then reopened the critic idea as a possible
bridge between independent MPPI and learner experience. The central open
question is whether the critic can evaluate the learner's own actions and
continuations when those differ from the planner's. Do not implement or launch
the SAC study below as an agreed next step. Critic-free alternatives using
direct longer/full-episode model rollout costs remain under discussion. Plain
return-weighted imitation alone does not establish optimization of the new
policy's own return.

Draft experiment design, 2026-10-24. No new training jobs are launched by this
document. The two 75-iteration Anchor/Selective runs completed unchanged in
their frozen checkout. Their final walking comparison remains a reference
experiment, not evidence for the hypothesis below.

**Question.** Does experience generated by a competent, independent MPPI
controller reduce the executed interaction budget needed for a standalone
reward-trained policy to walk reliably? A separate question is whether the
learner discovers useful behaviour earlier. Better final return alone does not
establish better exploration.

**Proposed loop.** Use the existing off-policy SAC implementation as the first
controlled test. Two independent experience sources feed one replay buffer:

1. The stochastic SAC policy executes its own episodes.
2. MPPI executes separate episodes using its own optimized receding-horizon
   plan and private sampling RNG. It receives no student nominal, student tail,
   student critic, or policy-matching cost. Its search settings do not depend on
   student competence. Keep the teacher's own plan between decisions; removing
   student influence does not mean resetting MPPI to a zero sequence.
3. Store actual `(observation, action, reward, next_observation, terminated)`
   transitions from both sources, with source and episode identifiers. Retain
   failures. Candidate planning rollouts are not silently counted as executed
   interaction data.
4. Train twin critics with SAC Bellman targets and train the actor to minimize
   `E[alpha * log pi(a|s) - min(Q1(s,a), Q2(s,a))]`. The target bootstrap uses
   the learner's own next action. There is no action-cloning or proximity loss
   in the primary method. A teacher action's short-horizon score does not
   determine whether the actor must copy it.
5. Repeat to a fixed executed-control budget and evaluate the saved neural
   policy alone. The learner is allowed to find actions better than its teacher.

This is discounted, entropy-regularized return optimization through an
approximate critic, not a guarantee of globally optimal behaviour. SAC is an
off-policy actor-critic, not PPO. If a PPO-specific study is wanted later, use
MPPI demonstrations for separately identified BC initialization/auxiliary loss;
compute PPO's likelihood ratios and advantages only from genuine learner
rollouts. Labeling MPPI actions with the old policy's log probabilities does
not make them on-policy.

**Critic bridge and support diagnostic.** The critic fits the actual replay
action: a teacher reward from `(s, a_teacher, r, s_next)` supervises
`Q(s, a_teacher)`, not `Q(s, a_actor)`. Bellman targets and actor updates then
query learner actions, which may lack support at teacher states. Different
behaviour distributions do not by themselves invalidate off-policy learning;
unsupported state-action queries, approximation and bootstrapping can make it
unreliable. An accurate `Q` for "teacher action, then current weak learner"
can also legitimately be low even though continuing MPPI would succeed.

Before choosing a remedy, save held-out full simulator states/solver warm
starts from both streams and freeze the actor, critics and entropy coefficient.
Compare teacher, actor and proposed-update first actions at each state, then
follow the current stochastic actor under repeated seeds. Calibrate bias,
overestimation and action-ranking accuracy with matching soft-return, gamma
and absorbing-failure semantics. Do not substitute the teacher's continuation
return for the learner's. Eight-second raw returns truncate continuing soft Q;
at gamma .999 substantial discounted value remains beyond that time. Extend
the diagnostic horizon or quantify truncation; a tail from the same critic is
not independent ground truth. Twin-critic agreement is not a certificate.

Candidate remedies preserve teacher independence: retain learner experience,
restrain poorly supported actor updates, or collect learner continuations from
saved teacher states in the simulator, with those extra rollouts charged to
the model budget. These are distinct ablations, not automatic fixes or
authorization for a training launch.

**Evidence already in this repository.** Step 15's independent teacher produced
79–84% completion on noisy 400-control training episodes, while its state-only
cloned students stalled. That does not settle reward-based learning from the
same kind of experience. Step 25 found that removing the teacher's own plan
memory substantially reduced completion at the tested budgets. Keep that memory; SAC need
not reproduce the teacher's history-conditioned action distribution, since it
learns return from transitions instead of matching those actions. Off-policy
support and critic error remain possible failure modes.

Step 19 already implemented the two-stream SAC loop. Only its actor-tail arm
was run; the matched plain-SAC and independent-teacher arms were never run.
That result cannot identify whether independent MPPI experience helps.

**Long-return setting.** The current SAC default is `gamma=.995`, with a
characteristic discount time near 1.6 seconds at `dt=.008`; an outcome eight
seconds later has weight `.995**1000`, about .00665. For this new study,
preregister `.999` in every primary arm: the characteristic time is about
eight seconds and the eight-second discount is about .368. This is a
continuing discounted objective, not an exact finite eight-second objective.

Expose gamma explicitly in the future runner and propagate it into the
environment adapter, replay rewards, targets, checkpoints and evaluation
metadata. The existing absorbing-failure convention must change consistently:
`-gamma * 5 / (1 - gamma)` is -4995 at .999, rather than -995 at .995. Regenerate
teacher transitions or recompute rewards from preserved raw costs and
termination flags. Do not reuse old adjusted rewards unchanged. Compare the
new arms to each other under identical rewards; do not attribute a difference
from an old .995 run solely to demonstrations. Primary physical metrics remain
eight-second survival, time alive, speed MAE and common finite-horizon task
cost; adjusted returns from different gamma settings are not interchangeable.

**Primary comparison.** Use six paired training seeds, a fixed maximum of
1,000,000 executed training controls per arm/seed, and checkpoints every
25,000 controls. These are proposed values to freeze before launch, not a
power guarantee. Do not conclude futility at 200k merely because the old SAC
ladder needed substantially more experience to walk.

| Arm | Executed interaction allocation | Learning rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | 100% learner stream | SAC from scratch |
| B | 75% learner, 25% independent MPPI | Identical SAC, shared replay |
| C, secondary data-source control | 75% learner, 25% independent random-action stream | Identical SAC, same collection infrastructure and quota |

The 25% is an executed-transition quota, not a demo minibatch weight. Use
10,000-control blocks, so B executes 7,500 learner controls and 2,500 teacher
controls. All arms receive the same gross executed budget and the same update
schedule per gross transition, including common warm-up rules. Keep replay
sampling uniform initially. Match architecture, initialization, optimizer,
replay capacity, physics, reward, reset distribution and learner update ratio.
Separate recorded RNG streams by role. Pair method seeds intentionally; do not
reuse a teacher sampling stream as a learner action RNG.

For the first trial, teacher and random-worker episodes are capped at 500
controls (four seconds), and learner episodes at 1,000 controls (eight seconds).
These collection caps are sampling cutoffs in a continuing task, not falls.
Preserve the final pre-reset next observation and bootstrap at healthy cutoffs;
only a physical termination masks the critic target. Record cutoff reasons.
Qualification and standalone evaluation still run for 1,000 controls. The
four-second teacher data window does not by itself demonstrate eight-second
teacher competence, hence the separate qualification panel.

B versus A is the primary independent-teacher effect. C helps distinguish
purposeful planning experience from a generic extra collection stream. A
policy-tail arm or a policy-seeded arm would be separate coupling ablations,
with all other settings matched; neither belongs in the independent arm.
Existing frozen Anchor/Selective runs are contextual references with different
training objectives and budgets, not matched controls for this study.

**Teacher qualification before training.** Start from the previously competent
raw settings `K=32`, `H=48`, frozen temperature 3, configured first-decision
refinements and its own shifted plan. The H32 default was materially weaker;
do not silently use it as an equivalent independent expert. On a separately
reserved development panel, measure the standalone teacher for 1,000 controls
per episode. Freeze its configuration and a competence criterion before
collecting trial data. A proposed pilot criterion is at least 60% eight-second
completion and at most .50 m/s speed MAE over 30 episodes. Passing that pilot
does not mean it meets the learner's stricter reliable-walking gate. If it
fails, improve/diagnose the teacher before the main trial and disclose the
selection cost; do not compensate by making it follow a weak student.

An independence check must replay the same simulator state, solver warm start,
MPPI plan memory and planner RNG under different student checkpoints and get
the same teacher actions/scores. Teacher episode collection must not load an
actor. Teacher traces retain source, task/config hashes and planner provenance.

**Short-horizon improvement is not long-return dominance.** Two problems must
stay distinct. First, finite stochastic MPPI synthesis can produce a weighted
mean plan whose scored cost is worse than its starting plan. Second, even an
exact minimizer of the short-horizon score can sacrifice a useful preparation
action whose payoff lies beyond that horizon. The current 100-step policy tail
does not remove this truncation problem. It is therefore not sound to make
the learner always imitate MPPI, or to label an action globally superior merely
because its short score is lower. In the proposed loop, MPPI contributes
transitions; the learner's long-return critic and actor objective choose the
policy's actions. Their estimates can be wrong, so improvement remains an
empirical hypothesis.

A separately identified teacher diagnostic could retain the lowest-cost plan
among its own nominal, sampled candidates and re-simulated weighted mean, all
under the same initial state and score. This guarantees no worse scored plan
than its nominal in that simulator, not a better whole-episode policy. Use
MPPI's own nominal, not the student. This changes the teacher action-selection
rule and must not be silently bundled into the primary raw-MPPI replay test.

**Exploration measurements.** Record actor-originated and teacher-originated
experience separately. Preregister the first actor episode that completes
eight seconds with MAE <=.30 m/s, the first stable forward-locomotion segment,
and actor-only healthy-state/contact-mode coverage. Fix coverage features,
normalization, bins and any subsampling rules from physical ranges or the
qualification pilot before comparing learners. Exclude absolute forward
position and elapsed time from novelty features. Report rare falling states
separately from useful coverage; extra teacher states are not evidence that
the actor explores better. Compare coverage both at equal actor-transition
counts and equal gross interaction budgets. Test whether teacher successes
precede newly successful actor trajectories, without claiming that temporal
ordering alone proves a causal mechanism.

Dense-reward Walker is an initial feasibility task. A broader exploration
claim needs a separately specified sparse/delayed-reward or harder
contact-discovery task, with an independently competent teacher and matched
baselines. Do not alter rewards during the primary Walker trial or generalize
from one environment as though it establishes broad exploration superiority.

**Sample-efficiency and walking outcomes.** At fixed budgets, evaluate the
deterministic policy alone on common development resets, with all failures
retained. The joint working gate is >=90% eight-second survival AND mean
speed MAE <=.30 m/s. Use a fixed 100-reset development panel for milestone
decisions; define time-to-gate as the first of two consecutive checkpoint
evaluations passing both criteria. Report first passage, sustained passage,
final-budget performance and learning-curve area. Runs that never pass remain
in the analysis as censored/failing runs; do not drop them. Keep evaluation
cost in the compute ledger, outside learner replay.

Define common finite eight-second task cost explicitly. For an episode that
falls after `n` executed controls, use `sum(executed raw task costs) +
5 * (1000 - n)`, retaining the configured falling-transition/terminal charge
once. A healthy full-horizon timeout has no absorbing suffix. Existing
evaluation `total_cost` is only the executed prefix and cannot be used alone
as this comparable eight-second metric. Gamma-adjusted return is a separate
secondary quantity. Do not add this finite evaluation suffix to replay rewards,
which already use the continuing discounted failure convention.

Use the predeclared final-budget checkpoint for the final comparison, not a
best checkpoint selected from final outcomes. Report every training seed,
paired seed-level differences and uncertainty that resamples training seeds
as well as matched reset cases. Reset intervals alone are not training-seed
robustness. Choose/audit training, monitoring, qualification and final reset
panels before launch; no new IDs are allocated by this draft. Reserved resets
5000000–5000199 stay untouched until a separately locked qualification stage.
Human-demo comparison is conditional on having an actual recorded teleoperation
dataset; report its acquisition transitions and human time. Do not claim to
beat teleoperation without that arm.

**Resource accounting.** Plot outcomes against (1) gross executed training
controls, including all teacher/demo acquisition and failures, (2) actor-only
controls, (3) planner/model simulated controls, and (4) wall time. The main
sample-efficiency axis is gross executed controls. Count all MPPI horizon
steps, refinement passes, continuation rollouts, actual prefetched acquisition
and qualification/tuning costs in separate ledger fields. Log source quotas
and learner update counts; each checkpoint must account for acquired-but-not-
yet-consumed transitions. A reused teacher corpus may additionally have a
clearly labeled amortized-cost view, but each learner's primary accounting
includes acquiring its own corpus. All current environments are MuJoCo, so
these measurements establish simulator interaction efficiency under a supplied
model, not demonstrated savings on physical robot trials or learned-model data.

**Implementation boundary.** Most of the data flow already exists in
`scripts/training/train_walker2d_sac.py`, `src/rl/mppi_stream.py` and
`src/rl/sac.py`. The relevant existing independent flags are
`--mppi-share 0.25 --mppi-terminal none --mppi-K 32 --mppi-H 48`.
They do not yet implement this full protocol. Future work must expose gamma,
add source-tagged transition/coverage and acquisition ledgers, add the random
stream control and protocol/report runner, validate termination/truncation and
reward consistency, implement the explicit finite-horizon cost metric, and
implement the independence check. Run brief meaningful
plumbing tests in a separate checkout before launching any main trial. None of
these changes should be copied into the active frozen 75-iteration checkout.

**Claim limits and sources.** SAC with demonstration data is not itself a new
algorithm. This study tests the value of independent planner-generated
experience with explicit budget accounting. An exploration claim additionally
requires improved actor discovery/coverage; a physical sample-efficiency claim
requires physical acquisition evidence. Sources:
[SAC](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.01290),
[demonstration-augmented policy gradients](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss14/p49.pdf),
[PPO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.06347), and the repository's
[experiment history](experiment_log.md) (Steps 15, 19 and 25).
