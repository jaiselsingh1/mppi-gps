Neither diagnosis trains a policy or changes trajectory optimization. H5.0 subsequently tested proposal guidance during acquisition; its fixed-update learning contrast was not reached. The earlier [S1.2 visitation draft](student_visitation_s1_2_hypothesis.md) remains preserved and unlaunched.

## Formulations and open hypotheses

[Primary sources](loop_v3_prior_art.md) and the [adaptive-coupling review](adaptive_coupling_review_v1.md) explain what alternating optimization, post-learning evaluation and GPS already establish. [Paper mapping](paper_mapping.md) states the departures of this direct torque policy from the original state-to-state formulation. Current finite candidate reweighting has no inherited convergence guarantee.

[H3.2](loop_v3_next_hypothesis_v3_2.md) is an unlaunched inherited-network input diagnostic. It is distinct from the executed [fresh L1 pipeline](student_learnability_track_v1.md). [H4.2](adaptive_coupling_h4_2_hypothesis.md) is an untested proposal for parent-relative learner-response selection. Neither document authorizes an experiment or establishes that its mechanism helps.

[H5.0](teacher_proposal_h5_0_hypothesis.md) tested a guided proposal mechanism beyond completed-bank selection. Its [audited result](teacher_proposal_h5_0_round1_results.md) failed the frozen teacher condition before learning could be measured. The [protocol](teacher_proposal_h5_0_execution_protocol.md), [authorized recipe](../configs/studies/teacher_proposal_h5_0_round1.json) and [metric review](teacher_proposal_h5_0_metric_review.md) preserve the distinction between descriptive motion distance, conditional agreement and fixed-update usefulness. The original preparation and launch snapshots remain unchanged.

[C1.0](student_consequence_c1_execution.md) now has an implemented, authorized bounded execution contract: one consequence-weighted fitting loss against uniform and stratum-shuffled controls, using all 12 qualified independent episodes. Its [original review protocol](student_consequence_c1_protocol.md) remains unchanged. All prerequisites passed and its fixed roster completed, with zero walking/full survival in every actor group. The [result](student_consequence_c1_results.md) preserves passed saved scientific checks and a failed audit coordinator; the original outer closure remains failed. [Separate audit-only recovery](student_consequence_c1_audit_recovery_results.md) is resolved through A2; the first recovery failure is also preserved. No scientific retry or S1.2 launch ran.
