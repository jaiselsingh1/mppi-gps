## Three distinct measurements

| Quantity | Representation and reduction | Purpose and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| K1.2 motion distance R | Saved physical height, seven wrapped angles and nine raw velocities; fixed SI scales and ordered 25-control windows; root translation and clock removed | Describes finite local motion differences. It is neither a task cost nor a teaching gate. No periodic walking or particular reference path is required. |
| Existing conditional agreement D | At each candidate's own pre-action state, native 17-component clipped observation followed by the fixed W1 normalization; sum of six squared bounded native-torque differences, then unweighted sum over the executable supported prefix | Encourages local action compatibility with the current parent on the candidate's own induced states. It does not evaluate the student after learning, measure recovery, or certify teachability. |
| Fixed-update usefulness | Fresh standalone walking and full survival after exactly 2,000 continuation updates, alongside teacher competence and separately reported cost/reconstruction | Decides whether the resulting demonstrations improved the student under this fitting procedure. This is the admission outcome, not an inner differentiable teacher objective. |

This comparison keeps D's representation, action scale, timestep reduction, support rule, horizon H=48, coefficient alpha=0.1199341983507337, temperature tau=3 and final plan proximity eta_u=0.01 fixed. There is no division by six or H, no dt factor, temporal reweighting, recurrence penalty, metric sweep or distance chosen to manufacture a ranking. The supported prefix includes the fall-causing action and excludes subsequent rows; the current support radius is null. Finite observations, finite native candidate costs and the native healthy-prefix mask are required. Native observations omit global root-x, use the existing position coordinates and clip the nine velocities to [-10,10] before normalization.

## Controlled change and protected execution

Each decision obtains two K=256, H=48 proposal streams. Both use two optimization passes after reset and one on later decisions, with the existing noise covariance, second-order temporal filter and bounded perturbation update. The protected producer receives no actor, selected guided plan or coupling callback. Its nominal, RNG, initial-plan flag and caches are separate from the second stream; the selected plan never writes back to that protected state.

Before each decision, preserve both producers' private nominal/RNG/initial-plan state in a separately bound context group. Preserve both final-pass cache weight vectors after planning. Physical cache arrays and each last plan resolve to canonical native-query evidence rather than duplicated rollouts. The unexpected-error path reserves one additional full simulator-context group, at most2KiB; it does not authorize retrying the paid operation.

For the second stream, P uses Q=J+alpha D/2 during every sampled optimization pass. The policy is the fixed incoming parent throughout round one. I and S measure the same D as observer work but return the unchanged native score, so their perturbation weights and next nominal remain task-only. Thus guidance in P changes its weighted nominal and subsequent proposal distribution, including its shifted nominal at the next actual decision. States delivered by the native hook are post-action: D must reconstruct pre-action states from the initial context plus the preceding saved post states. No pre-action/post-action substitution is allowed.

| Arm | Protected producer | Second producer | Final selector |
|---|---|---|---|
| I: independent | Native J | Native J; D observer discarded | Native J plus common plan proximity |
| S: selector-only | Native J | Native J; D observer discarded | Native J plus common proximity and alpha D/2 |
| P: proposal-guided | Native J | Q inside each nominal update | Same selector as S |

Both stream means and the baseline/guided selector means are separately simulated before use. I performs the guided selector/verification work only as a discarded observer. All arms therefore reserve the same calls and maximum neural rows; realized supported rows or failures may differ. Observer work is not claimed as efficient computation. The protected producer never sees observer results. Different successful motions remain eligible.

Every final candidate and synthesized mean must satisfy J(U)<=1.05 J(U_protected)+1e-12, with U_protected the finite native minimum-J protected plan, including its separately verified mean. The same guard applies to every arm. A rejected or unsupported synthesized mean falls back to the protected plan; an unsupported policy action is never executed. This local guard cannot guarantee full-episode competence. Candidate ties use immutable order: protected samples 0–255, protected verified mean, second samples 0–255, second verified mean. A mean replaces a candidate only on strictly lower score. Unexpected nonfinite actor outputs, ownership errors or numerical revocation invalidate the declared contrast and stop paid execution after evidence is persisted.
