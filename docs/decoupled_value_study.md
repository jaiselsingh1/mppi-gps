Status: four-arm, 15-iteration seed-0 pilot authorized. This records the user's decisions before outcomes. The priority is a working, falsifiable algorithm; compute ledgers support later interpretation.

## Fixed comparisons

| Arm | Proposals | Synthesis / selection | Continuation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline-Anchor | 256 policy-centred samples | Verified mean, established Anchor regression | Explicit 100-control policy tail |
| Baseline-Selective | 256 policy-centred samples | Verified mean, established Selective weights | Explicit 100-control policy tail |
| Split-Medoid | 128 policy-centred + 128 independent samples | Verified mean, cached improving discrete fallback, Selective weights | Explicit 100-control policy tail |
| Split-Medoid-Critic | Same split | Verified synthesis/fallback plus strict value-change gate | Learned value of actual current policy; no candidate policy-tail rollouts |

All arms start from the identical random seed-0 `17→256→256→6` tanh network, use one planner update (`M=1`), K=256/H=32, and execute only the noisy policy during collection. The independent half uses a zero/neutral nominal and fixed sigma=0.3; the policy-centred scale is `0.3/sqrt(1+k/25)`. No hybrid `min(default, policy)` tail or additional search pass is allowed. Any different independent nominal, fallback distance penalty, tie-breaking, critic optimizer or target-update setting must be fixed in the prelaunch configuration.

Collection remains ten episodes of at most 400 controls, execution noise 0.1, labelling every second visited control. Each arm visits its own policy's states under matched reset/noise seed schedules; these are not identical datasets after the policies diverge. At each iteration use a **common logical query count across all four new arms**, no greater than 2,000 and rounded down to a multiple of 64 from the minimum available state count. Select queries deterministically before candidate outcomes. Preserve zero-query/zero-weight stalls rather than fabricating labels.

Shared policy fitting: ESS target 0.2K; two-iteration replay; 2,000 requested Adam updates; batch 256; learning rate 0.0003; zero weight decay. Freeze stored labels, old-policy actions, gates and weights. Anchor retains its existing equality semantics (only strictly worse proposals are replaced with the old action); Selective requires strict improvement. Empty positive-weight replay skips fitting and records unchanged policy hash and zero applied updates. Do not relax a critic gate to force learning. Disable the heavy four-controller research ladder and automatic health-based early aborts for this fixed pilot, except numerical failures, which must stop cleanly and remain reported.

## Verification and actual-policy value

Before Walker execution, the exact saddle test must show that candidates `(1,1)` and `(1,-1)` cost zero, their mean `(1,0)` costs 2, the policy `(0,0)` costs 1, and fallback selects an action costing below 0.1. In Walker, every synthesized trajectory is verified against the policy nominal using the same objective. A local Euclidean ball cannot guarantee a single basin. Use an already scored improving discrete candidate when the mean fails; its cached score avoids another search or synthesis rollout. The specified cost-plus-action-distance winner is an exemplar, not necessarily a geometric medoid.

The user selected **actual-policy value**, not accepted-plan/hybrid targets. Fit the critic from fresh deterministic closed-loop rollouts of the named current policy, with gamma=0.995 and consistent H-step discounted Bellman targets. Planner-selected trajectories must not be labelled as trajectories of a policy that did not execute those actions. Record target-network and policy snapshot order. The strict future-value decrease compares critic snapshots at identical recorded successor states: it is an estimated decrease, not a direct measurement of improved future policy outcomes. Fitting error or a strict gate may reject all labels; retain gate coverage, residuals and stalls.

Implementation frozen before launch: cost value `17→256→256→softplus(1)`, Adam learning rate0.0003, 2,000 updates/batch256, normalized TD(H) loss plus equal-weight remaining-policy-return anchors, frozen targets within each fit and hard synchronization afterward. Each iteration acquires ten deterministic current-policy episodes of at most1,000 controls on resets `10100000+100k+e`; eight fit the value and two are held out. A true fall supplies the exact discounted absorbing continuation `5/(1−gamma)` after the falling transition; healthy cutoffs bootstrap from the frozen previous value and are explicitly estimated. Cold start requires actual absorbing return anchors and initializes cost scale from their mean. The actor gate is `tanh(max(V_previous(x_H)−V_current(x_H),0)/1)`, zero for fallen endpoints after initialization. Iteration0 explicitly bypasses this additional gate because no previous grounded value exists; that update is not described as value-gated. No later bypass or gate relaxation is allowed. The critic fit and scoring precede that iteration's actor update.

A discounted continuing value is not the baselines' undiscounted finite T=100 policy tail. The critic contrast therefore changes the continuation objective/estimator as well as backward credit and gating. Discount prefix and boundary terms consistently within that arm. If the final runner also checks nominal/mean/selected-fallback plans with an explicit T=100 realized tail to reject bad critic labels, freeze that rule before execution and ledger the overhead; it must not become an unreported K-candidate tail or post hoc result-dependent change.

Falls charge the actual falling transition and terminal cost once, then the appropriate absorbing continuation once. Administrative cutoffs are not falls. Critic targets/calibration must distinguish them and report missing-return semantics. No teacher/critic prediction is treated as a certificate of long-horizon standalone improvement.

## Budget decision and accounting

The user chose a **candidate-rollout cap**, retaining K=256 and at most 2,000 logical labelled states, rather than an all-operation 67.6M ceiling. Candidate planning for the first three arms is bounded by `2000×256×(32+100)=67,584,000` logical controls per iteration. Their common query count and cached discrete fallback preserve matching candidate-planning work. The critic arm removes the K explicit T=100 tails; its value fitting and any selected-plan verification remain separately visible.

Report all overhead separately: live collection, policy-nominal generation, nominal/mean verification, fallback checks, probes, actual-policy value-target acquisition, critic calibration, standalone evaluation, and rendering. Existing GPU buffers advance padded capacity worlds, and postfall worlds may still advance physically. Also report wall/GPU time and requested/applied optimizer updates. Do not call candidate-budget matching identical total compute, identical datasets, or demonstrated sample efficiency.

## Claim boundaries

Split-Medoid changes proposals and fallback together; without a fifth seeded-medoid arm it cannot identify which mechanism caused a difference or establish that policy seeding is essential. No fifth arm is authorized. Split proposals remain policy-dependent through their policy-centred half and continuation. The critic arm changes objective/estimation/gating together; a difference does not isolate dynamic programming. This is one paired-seed early pilot, not an exploration, human-demonstration, equal-budget RL, cross-seed robustness or sample-efficiency result. Preserve adverse outcomes and numerical stops. New arms, gate relaxation, extra iterations, tuning or seed changes require a new named scope decision.
