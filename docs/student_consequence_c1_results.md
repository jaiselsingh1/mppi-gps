## Formulation and controls

The [original review protocol](student_consequence_c1_protocol.md) and [authorized execution contract](student_consequence_c1_execution.md) retain the frozen science. Scientific worker source was commit `56b737413884c031bb65bcc331b48ed700e59540`; later reporting and launcher repairs are separately bound postprocessing code. All source/replay, continuation, seed, capacity and accounting prerequisites passed before their dependent work. All twelve independent source teachers walked and survived their complete 1,000 controls under the original byte-exact restoration and numerical replay tolerances.

For each source row i, C1 restored its complete simulator context, substituted its already saved incoming-parent action once, then executed the recorded teacher suffix for h_i=min(100,1000−t_i) controls. Its effect was

\[
\Delta_i=C_{h_i}(x_i;\pi_0(o_i),u^T_{i+1:i+h_i-1})-C_{h_i}(x_i;u^T_{i:i+h_i-1}).
\]

The single frozen weighting mechanism was

\[
\tilde w_i=1+\min(9,\max(0,\Delta_i)/20),\quad
w_i=\tilde w_i/\operatorname{mean}_{j=1}^{4000}\tilde w_j,
\qquad
L_w=\frac{1}{6|B|}\sum_{i\in B}w_i\|\pi_\theta(o_i)-u_i^T\|^2.
\]

Weights were computed once in float64 and frozen as actual float32 loss weights. All three treatment vectors were nonconstant and distinct from their one declared stratum-shuffled control. Uniform loss and the shuffled weight multiset were retained. Each three-arm contrast started from the same complete retained parent, normalization, Adam and RNG state and used the same minibatch stream. No parent was refitted. Targets, width128 torque interface, features, optimizer and 2,000-update budget remained fixed; all children executed their six torques directly.

This changes fitting only. Independent MPPI discovery remains available in the project but no planner ran here, no new teacher was acquired, and no learner feedback changed trajectory optimization. It is neither adaptive coupling nor a CIO–GPS reproduction. The torque actor differs from CIO’s state-to-state execution/controller formulation. A fixed open-loop suffix effect is not feedback cost-to-go, recovery ability or a certificate of teachability.

## Causal verdict and next decision

Established observations are successful replaying teachers, nondegenerate weights, matched completed fits, zero walking/full survival across all parents and children, and a passing saved scientific receipt followed by failed coordinator closure. Within those limits, the planned benefit of this one stale scalar weighting rule did not occur. Uniform fitting also failed walking transfer under this continued fitter; that comparison does not isolate the new corpus from the extra updates.

Coverage, sampling frequency, useful sequential targets and the effects of errors after the policy changes remain unresolved. C1 does not show that cost-aware learning generally fails, that rare states are important, or that action similarity certifies teachability. Its fixed scalar weights do not test trajectory-structured learning. Prior corrective-data failures remain counterevidence to assuming coverage is an established remedy.

**Next decision:** review an explicitly authorized audit-only closure/recovery protocol for these preserved outputs before another scientific launch; do not silently rerun C1. The active software repair and synthetic tests are complete, but the original outer gate remains failed. After that decision, the single scientific recommendation is to version the existing [S1.2 fixed-label visitation draft](student_visitation_s1_2_hypothesis.md) for review: the same reference label function on equal-size learner-visited versus reference-visited states, with matched complete incoming continuation and fixed 2,000 updates. Bind a finite nonselective label/context validity check first; a reference that walks on its own states is not assumed to recover at learner states. No acquisition, fitting, coupling or follow-up was launched automatically.
