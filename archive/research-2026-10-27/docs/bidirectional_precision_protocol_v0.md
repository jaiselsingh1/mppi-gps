# Prospective Ending precision-stage learner assay

This is a separate finite development test after the v3 negative result. The full v3 outcome, source, budget and failed child-learning gate remain unchanged. No success is claimed in advance.

V3 established a useful online improvement on its development task: exact frozen-actor continuations let protected independent MPPI select better first actions, with 64/64 captures and lower complete cost than the actor alone. Its next learner failed: both matched child actors had higher deterministic complete cost than the parent, and guided-data child cost was slightly worse than independent-data child cost. All had 64/64 deterministic capture and 44/64 stochastic capture. Successful teaching trajectories did not establish successful learning from them.

A focused hypothesis is that broad stochastic training behavior and deterministic deployment create different local refinement objectives. The successful actor retains action standard deviation near .3 during own-return learning, while the controller and guided teacher use its deterministic mean. Buying extra capture margin under broad action noise can increase preparation cost of the already successful mean controller. This is a hypothesis to check against saved components and gate margins, not an established causal explanation.

Test one competence-conditioned precision stage. Load the exact frozen v3 round-0 parent policy, optimizer and RNG state. Preserve the original arm. For the precision arm change only the initial trainable log standard deviation to log(.01), once; preserve the mean network, optimizer and RNG. The variance remains trainable thereafter. The .01 setting is a new explicit development choice, informed by the task's action-to-ending sensitivity and saved actor margins; it was not in v3. It is not a universal exploration setting.

Require fresh complete deterministic parent competence first. Use the same already executed independent and guided acquisition traces from v3, with complete provenance and paid ancestry. They are auxiliary data, never relabeled returns for the new actor. In each variance arm acquire 64 fresh complete stochastic actor-owned episodes: eight ordinary and eight acquired contexts, four replicates each. Match underlying normal random draws across the variance arms. Within each arm, clone the complete parent/optimizer/RNG into raw-data and guided-data children; share the exact own-return data and optimizer schedule. Each gets the same 100 common teacher rows plus 100 successful arm-specific teacher rows, with an explicit shared fallback where independent acquisition found no success. Use the unchanged four-epoch owned-MC update and auxiliary weight .1. There are no extra supervision steps.

Evaluate parent and both children on 64 fresh paired ordinary roots, separately under deterministic deployment and stochastic training behavior. A precision-stage benefit requires the guided-data child to beat both its precision parent and raw-data sibling in complete mean cost by at least .001, without capture regression in either execution mode and with at least 90% deterministic capture. Report the unchanged-variance arm regardless of its result. No planner guidance is deployed during this assay. Passing it would justify a new full feedback formulation, not retroactively complete v3 or establish the original physical-task thesis.

Before launch, freeze a fixed maximum of 22,800 actual scalar controls, zero model controls, 77,200 actor inference rows, 112 Adam calls and four diagnostic backward traversals. Those are conservative ceilings; unused reservations are not expenditure. No automatic retry, extra updates or variance search. Source, parent/checkpoint/archive inputs and all executed episodes are hash bound. Count previous discovery, controller qualification and acquisition as shared paid ancestry in each condition; never describe reused data as free discovery or claim efficiency from this assay.

Fresh seed roles: parent competence 109,000–109,015; own ordinary contexts 110,000–110,007; evaluation 111,000–111,063; evaluation noise 112,000. Acquired states are fixed retained independent-discovery states at controls 6, 12, 18 and 21. Old held outcomes supply no new targets or teacher labels. Analysis may produce another separately specified formulation if this test fails.
