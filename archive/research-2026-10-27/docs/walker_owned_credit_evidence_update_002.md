# Walker evidence update: variation is not a useful learning direction

This is an additive read-only checkpoint. Earlier studies, frozen sources and
negative outcomes remain unchanged. New simulation, actor inference, actor
updates and critic fits are all zero. The fresh acquisition qualification is
still pending, so no physical learning diagnostic has started.

## What the old Walker records can actually establish

Option1 retains 200 pairs of logged complete-return aggregates for a
synthesized ES proposal and one sample selected by a prefix-plus-base-critic
surrogate. These two proposals were subsequently evaluated on common training
and held-out contexts. The archived evaluator and driver match the recorded
source hashes and execute each candidate's complete remaining continuation.
However, those changed candidates have no saved per-transition raw traces or
query identities. The new audit checks the metric arithmetic and source
contract; it does not independently replay or reconstruct their returns.

Across all 200 updates, ES improved both logged training and held-out scores
zero times. Selected samples improved both scores three times. During the
last 120, ordinary-start-only updates, pair ordering agreed across the two
panels in 116/120 cases. ES beat the selected sample on each panel in 91/120
cases, but still never jointly improved the base. A consistent ranking between
two poor proposals therefore does not establish a useful optimization direction.
The different sampled and ES parameter radii also prevent treating this pair
comparison as a controlled test of continuation ownership.

No complete-return pair exists for two original members of the 64-candidate
sample bank. There is also no saved exact-base continuation for every candidate
prefix. Thus the records cannot establish full-bank coverage or separate
continuation mismatch from critic approximation. The complete-return ranking
repair remains justified as a missing mechanism to test, rather than a proven
way to learn walking.

Saved standalone action traces show that the deployed actor actually changed:
initial and final runs have exactly matching 100 initial full states, warm
starts and reset seeds, with all 100 first actions changed and mean action L2
difference 0.246301. No saved initial or final action coordinate reaches
absolute value 0.95. These observations rule out an unchanged executed actor
and actuator saturation on those saved executions. They say nothing about
missing perturbation-specific action changes or whether those perturbations
were scaled usefully. The final policy still fell in all 100 episodes.

A single future antithetic pair could falsify action effectiveness or reveal
return variation. It could not establish repeatable improvement, optimizer
scalability or the usable-learning-signal gate. Any eventual physical assay
must distinguish those requirements and preserve full candidate-owned traces.
No such protocol is frozen or launched at this pending first gate.
