# Prospective own-occupancy long-credit falsifier

This new finite development formulation follows the retained negative Q-filter assay. Its independently audited positive action labels were dominated by rescue states outside the saved ordinary-state range. A correct old-policy action preference at one state does not ensure that supervised fitting improves the deployed policy elsewhere. The next test therefore learns an action-gradient signal on the current student's actual state occupancy. It does not copy a short-horizon MPPI action or fit a learned terminal critic.

## What is learned, and whose future is evaluated

The learned object remains the bounded 128×128 action policy. Start from the exact precision001 parent, including optimizer and private RNG, with its original 64 complete stochastic own episodes. Their Monte Carlo costs-to-go and leave-one-trajectory-out baselines retain the original stochastic parent's ownership. They are reused data, not new returns of a child.

Execute the frozen deterministic mean parent on 16 new ordinary roots, 181000–181015, for all 25 controls. Preserve the actual pre-action states, observations, mean actions and complete costs. At every one of those 400 states, measure two complete simulator continuations: first apply clip(mean − .05) or clip(mean + .05), then follow that same frozen deterministic parent to the true terminal at 25. Both branches are paid even when they share actions or endpoints. All later actor actions are recomputed at their branch's actual states. These are explicit counterfactual model continuations; neither is called an executed child return.

Compute a finite difference g=(Q_plus−Q_minus)/(a_plus−a_minus). The full-credit arm uses complete branch cost. The local-credit arm uses only the first min(4, remaining) recorded costs from those same paid branches. The null arm uses zero. Keep complete raw branch costs, query references, terminal outcomes and the actual center-parent suffix cost. Record finite-difference curvature and threshold crossings descriptively; they cannot change the fixed perturbation or select training rows. Include post-gate action-invariant rows, whose measured slopes should be zero.

Use fixed evenly spaced indices to select 200 of the 400 rows. Each of three exact parent clones uses the unchanged four-epoch own-return PPO update, plus .1 times mean[(mean_theta−saved_mean) * stop_gradient(g)/25]. This extra objective is a measured action-gradient surrogate, not a teacher-action target. The null arm still executes the same zero-signal forwards and sampler draws. All arms start with the same optimizer/RNG and use the same original own-return dataset and update schedule. Do not reset inherited Adam momentum or change the actor's action variance during this test. Those effects are controlled by the null arm.

The future in Q belongs to the frozen deterministic parent. It is distinct from the stochastic parent's complete MC targets, the changed actor, and an MPPI continuation. A finite perturbation across a discontinuous ending is a measured finite difference, not necessarily a derivative. A mixture of stale finite-difference directions and clipped stochastic policy gradients has no automatic monotonic-improvement guarantee. Actual changed-actor executions determine whether it works.

## Qualification and strong controls

Construct all 15 interpolation actors before any qualification forward: fractions {1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1} for the full, local and null proposals. Interpolate all trainable parameters, including log standard deviation, using the same exact endpoint and dtype rules as interpolation001. Preserve every outcome.

Qualify the parent and all 15 actors on 16 fresh ordinary roots, 182000–182015, separately in deterministic and stochastic modes with noise root 184000. A full-credit fraction is eligible only if complete mean cost improves by at least .001 over the parent and both same-fraction local/null proposals, capture does not regress in either mode, and deterministic capture is at least 90%. Choose minimum full-credit deterministic cost, with smaller fraction as tie break. Independently choose the lowest-cost local and null fractions that qualify against the parent in both modes; use the parent if none qualifies.

If no full-credit fraction qualifies, stop this formulation. Otherwise execute five controllers on 64 disjoint fresh roots, 183000–183063, in both modes with noise root 185000: parent, selected full-credit actor, independently selected local actor, independently selected null actor, and the previously qualified best uniform-data raw actor from interpolation001. Duplicate actors are still executed and charged. Final success requires at least .001 complete mean cost improvement over every reference, no capture regression in either mode, and at least 90% deterministic capture. Final outcomes cannot choose fractions or alter training.

Freeze hard maxima before launch: 39,600 scalar controls (10,400 model and 29,200 actual), 80,000 actor rows, 84 Adam steps and three diagnostic gradient calls. These include the 400 actual center controls, 10,400 complete branch controls, 12,800 qualification controls and 16,000 final controls. The pinned 1,144-row original MC corpus is expected to require 60 actual Adam steps, not 84; publish actual totals. Unused final reservations cannot fund retries, more rows, new perturbations or tuning. Record failed/unknown work and setup, execution and audit wall time. Original MPPI discovery, parent training, precision and interpolation remain paid shared ancestry. The local and null arms need not incur these model probes in an efficient standalone implementation; this shared-query causal experiment makes no efficiency claim.

## Interpretation and integration path

This is a falsifier of a long-credit learning mechanism on a cheap task whose parent already captures. A positive result would show cost refinement of an actual standalone actor using measured consequences beyond four controls. If full/local signals or resulting decisions coincide, do not claim evidence for a long-credit advantage. This run does not test new discovery, a physical robot, two beneficial feedback rounds or a guided-data advantage.

Only after this learning gate works should the bidirectional comparison collect different acquired states through raw versus qualified guided MPPI. The same frozen student must then execute its own suffixes from each acquired-state set; those new actual outcomes and measured continuation gradients train matched children. Common ordinary starts, acquired-state sampling rules, optimizer work and costs stay matched. Fresh whole-actor evaluation must favor the guided-data child, then a changed actor must re-earn influence through executed replanning comparisons. Repeat with new owner-correct data after promotion. Identical state occupancy in the present gradient test cannot substantiate that later data-feedback relationship.

The assumptions are explicit simulator restoration, accurate measured transitions on queried branches, enough useful discovered states to expose ending-sensitive choices, and policy generalization that survives fresh ordinary-root tests. A critic or finite difference cannot manufacture missing successful behavior. Prior art includes deterministic policy gradients (Silver et al., 2014, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v32/silver14.html) and rollout cost-sensitive policy improvement (Ross and Bagnell, 2014, https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5979). Neither that primitive nor actor-owned sampling is claimed as novel. The research object remains the protected, qualified MPPI–policy feedback loop.
