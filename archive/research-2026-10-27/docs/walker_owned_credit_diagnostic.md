# Walker: gated repair of actor-credit ownership

The research product remains a standalone walking policy and useful learned
feedback into independent MPPI. This stage does not redefine that objective as
critic accuracy, recovery from late states, or additional planner computation.
The ending-actor comparison is evidence that changed-actor execution and a
changed prefix followed by the frozen base actor are distinct objectives.

Current new-execution budget is **zero physics controls, zero planner calls,
zero actor updates, zero critic fits**. It permits raw-evidence inspection,
verified observation of existing jobs and synthetic implementation checks.
The existing fresh qualification already has a protocol and a live waiting
launcher; it must not be duplicated. A physical diagnostic budget, exact source,
candidate bank, roots and stopping rules must be frozen separately BEFORE any
new Walker execution, and only once the preceding gate is supported. This is
not a follow-on full learning protocol or authorization to retune a campaign.

## Gate 1: competent independent acquisition

The complete tuned file is K256/H48, lambda3, sigma.3, low-pass noise at
23.4375Hz with125Hz sampling, initial_iterations2. Execute MPPI's own weighted
mean and carry its own nominal plan/private RNG. No policy nominal, action
target, archive fragment, actor continuation or learned score belongs here.
The legacy `raw_teacher_config()` silently replaces K256 with K32. New code
loads and validates the complete tuned configuration directly and fails closed
on any mismatch; old helpers and studies remain unchanged.

Fresh Step32 qualification is predeclared on64 resets55000000–55000063,
planner seeds885000000+i, on robopil5090. ArmC must survive>=58/64 and have
mean episode speed MAE<=.30. Its three controls A/B1/B2 must also remain
recorded and charged. Observe completed records and the actual process; an
empty log is not a completed failure. A pending or incomplete panel cannot
qualify. A completed failed panel stops the research execution at this gate.

Existing Step32 records are outcome summaries, NOT guide trajectories. The
source saves no raw state/action/cost/warmstart/nominal/RNG sequence for raw
MPPI. A separate bounded and charged recovery/acquisition must provide those
fields before learning consumes reached states. Recovered replay is the same
observation with additional verification cost, not an independent episode.
Preserve failures instead of choosing only walking episodes. In-flight or
failed legacy queries omitted by its append-after-completion ledger remain an
explicit accounting limitation if the process is interrupted.

The historical raw K256 panel is19/20 with MAE.14805, but planner/backend
source hashes differ. It locates useful evidence and assumptions; it does not
replace fresh qualification. See `walker_actor_credit_evidence_audit.md`.

## Gate 2: ownership repair

The old Option1 learner generates64 antithetic parameter candidates. Each is
ranked by32 of its own actions followed by a Q of the frozen BASE actor. It
fully executes only the ES proposal and one surrogate-best sampled candidate
before acceptance. Thus complete actor outcomes merely veto two proposals;
they do not rank all discovered parameter candidates.

The repair is to obtain every candidate's own complete remaining execution,
then use these measured returns to rank the bank and generate proposals. A
query must carry candidate identity, context identity, full state plus solver
warmstart digest, root ancestry, remaining horizon, raw costs, fall/cutoff
flags, and a unique query reference. A return from the base actor, a different
snapshot or an unfinished healthy prefix is rejected. Failures include the
falling transition once and the finite absorbing unhealthy cost5 for every
remaining task control; WalkerRL's infinite reward tail is not the objective.

The exact formula is
`sum(gamma**t * raw_cost[t]) + gamma**n * finite_absorbing_cost(gamma,N-n,5)`
on a real fall, and just the complete measured sum at a healthy task boundary.
No learned/nearest-neighbor/teacher tail may replace missing candidate data.
All eligible sampled and synthesized changes obey the same declared parameter
bound. Complete-return acceptance on separate roots remains required even
after correct complete-return ranking.

The new module has no critic, teacher-action target or successful-actor input.
Its synthetic tests check the ownership and selection contract, not Walker
learning. It is not evidence that an unexecuted candidate bank contains a
useful actor or that a bounded optimizer scales to71,942 parameters.

## Subsequent gates stay unproven

The old64-wide actor has5,702 parameters. Per-coordinate sigma.025 produced
candidate norms around1.89, while every ES update clipped to.25; none of the
200 ES updates was accepted. The final actor fell in100/100 episodes. Critic
calibration also became extremely poor. These facts motivate isolating
proposal coverage, scaling, ownership and ranking; they do not prove any one
cause or justify importing the toy optimizer unchanged.

Successful frozen Anchor/Selective256-wide actors and SAC may demonstrate
executable capacity and continuation mismatch only. Their weights, actions,
parameter directions and nominal plans cannot initialize or teach the new
learner. A capacity witness proves its own architecture/transform class, not a
different small or frozen-feature class. Candidate action changes on acquired
observations can be measured before physics without using teacher actions.

Any later signal assay must name the independently initialized actor class,
actual candidate perturbation norms/action changes, complete-return selection,
root-disjoint verification, failed candidates, repeated training seeds and
hard paid-work cap. Improvement on late curriculum states is only that result;
it must transfer to original starts. Final standalone walking requires>=90%
eight-second survival AND<=.30m/s MAE in EACH predeclared training seed on
untouched panels, with uncertainty/falls disclosed. Reset replication is not
training-seed robustness.

Only after an actor demonstrates competence may its frozen complete
continuations rank independently generated MPPI proposals. Such a score owns
the stated intervention-plus-frozen-actor continuation; it is not automatically
the value of future MPPI replanning. Matched closed-loop enabled/disabled
feedback must verify that ranking transfers to executed control, with the
same independent proposals, archive and all costs charged. No current actor
result establishes useful global feedback or exploration/sample efficiency.

## Current deliverables and evidence status

`configs/walker_owned_credit_diagnostic.json` freezes this stage's bounds and
gates. `src/gps/walker_owned_credit.py` supplies candidate-owned complete-score
and configuration guards; its tests use synthetic evaluators only. A separate
qualification auditor accepts only a complete fresh panel, records every fall
and committed control cost, and labels missing raw acquisition explicitly.
The new diagnostic run directory retains source/configuration hashes,
read-only historical evidence copies and live-handle observations. No existing
run, actor checkpoint, rejected update or negative verdict is changed.

The previous ending-actor turn was progress: it produced raw executed evidence
that changed which Walker score needs repair. This Walker stage likewise must
be judged by authoritative artifacts, not by intended future launches. The
goal stays active while fresh qualification is pending; no standalone walking,
useful feedback or efficiency benefit is yet established by this repair.
