# Prospective exact-Q teacher-credit assay

This revision tests the retained v3 compression failure. Full executed teacher outcomes improved control, but the next guided-data actor did not beat its parent or the matched raw-data child. Precision001 and interpolation001 remain negative: the best independently selected raw-data step beat the guided-data step. Success on a trajectory does not establish that every action in that trajectory improves the student.

Use the exact precision001 parent, full optimizer/private RNG state, 64 complete stochastic parent-owned episodes, and both original 200-row auxiliary batches. Preserve their original query references and full paid lineage. No new actor-owned learning episodes are collected. Their actual Monte Carlo returns and behavior likelihoods remain owned by the original behavior actor; counterfactual measurements never replace these returns.

Before fitting, for every distinct actual teacher (state, action) pair, measure two complete deterministic frozen-parent continuations to the true Ending terminal at control 25: parent mean action followed by the parent, and the actual teacher action followed by the parent. Both branches are paid even when the actions coincide. Cache only byte-identical full physical state/time, action, task, family and frozen parent identity. At most 200 distinct pairs are possible in the original batches. Keep all 400 logical row aliases, including zero or negative improvements.

Set each actual teacher row's auxiliary weight to max(Q_parent_mean − Q_teacher_action, 0). Positive rows are resampled to 200 by fixed evenly spaced indices; retain the original weights and exact index map. If none is positive, retain a zero-weight auxiliary batch. These are measured preferences under the old deterministic parent's continuation on the queried states, not returns of a changed actor or proof of broad improvement. The auxiliary objective is a supervised surrogate. Its direction must survive fresh complete actor executions.

Clone the exact parent for raw and guided arms. Apply the unchanged four-epoch complete-return policy-gradient update plus weighted auxiliary loss, same data, optimizer work and initial RNG. Both positive auxiliary batches use identical sampler draw counts. If exactly one arm has zero total auxiliary weight, the unchanged learner skips its auxiliary sampling and later policy-gradient permutations may differ; explicitly report this limitation rather than claim identical realized minibatches.

Checkpoint both children and all five parameter interpolation fractions {1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1} before any new qualification forward. Qualify all ten candidates and the parent on 16 fresh roots, deterministic and stochastic separately. A guided fraction must improve complete mean cost by at least .001 over the parent and same-fraction filtered raw child, with no capture regression in either mode and at least 90% deterministic capture. Select minimum guided deterministic cost, smaller fraction breaking ties. Independently select the best filtered raw fraction that qualifies against the parent, with parent fallback.

If no guided fraction qualifies, stop this assay. Otherwise evaluate five actual actors on 64 disjoint fresh roots in both modes: parent, selected guided, filtered raw at that fraction, independently selected filtered raw, and the previously qualified best uniform-data raw actor from interpolation001. Charge duplicate controllers separately. Final success requires at least .001 complete mean cost improvement over all four references, no capture regression in either mode, and at least 90% deterministic capture. Final outcomes cannot select a step or change training.

Freeze maximum work before execution: 34,800 scalar controls, consisting of at most 10,000 model controls and 24,800 live controls; 61,800 actor rows; 56 Adam steps; two diagnostic gradient calls. Actual shorter suffixes and complete episodes may use less. Qualification uses 8,800 live controls; final evaluation uses 16,000. No retries, added fractions or budget recycling. Qualification roots 171000–171015/noise 173000; final roots 172000–172063/noise 174000. Preserve query, inference, optimizer, failed-work and wall-time ledgers, all source/configuration, actor versions and raw branch evidence. Shared ancestors are charged once per full lineage, never free.

Q-filtered imitation is established prior art (Nair et al., 2018, https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.10089). Here its labels are paid complete simulator continuations rather than a learned critic's estimates; that substitution is a component test, not a claim of a new primitive. A positive result would establish one credit/compression link on the cheap mechanism task. It would not establish two feedback rounds, new exploration, sample efficiency, physical Acrobot success or a Walker result.
