# Prospective outcome-qualified policy step assay

This finite development revision follows two retained negatives: v3 improved online control but failed matched-child learning, and precision001 failed to repair learning by reducing training noise. The original-noise arm retained capture but increased cost; both full low-noise child updates lost capture in 64/64 deterministic trials. This assay tests smaller actual policy updates without another optimizer step. It cannot complete the two-round MPPI–GPS thesis by itself.

Reuse the exact precision-arm parent and two child checkpoints from the closed precision001 run. Pin their byte hashes and full paid lineage. Before inspecting any new outcome, construct five interpolation fractions: 1/16, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2 and 1. For both children interpolate all trainable actor parameters, including log standard deviation, from the common parent toward that child's complete-return-plus-auxiliary proposal. Use float64 parameter arithmetic followed by the original dtype; copy endpoints exactly. Architecture and action buffers must match, and parent private sampling state is preserved. There is no optimizer, new fitting, model rollout or planner in this assay.

Qualify all ten actors on the same 16 fresh roots, separately under deterministic mean deployment and stochastic behavior with matched normal draws. Evaluate the parent in both modes too. Preserve every outcome. A guided fraction is eligible only if complete mean cost improves by at least .001 over the parent and the same-fraction raw-data child, capture does not regress in either mode, and deterministic capture is at least 90%. Select the eligible fraction with the lowest guided deterministic complete cost, with smaller fraction as tie break.

Independently select the best raw-data fraction among all five raw candidates that meet the same cost/capture rules against the parent in both modes. If none qualifies, its controller is the unchanged parent. This stronger control prevents a favorable guided step size from being mistaken for better teaching data. It adds no qualification executions because all raw candidates are already tested.

If a guided fraction qualifies, evaluate four controllers on 64 new roots: parent, guided child at its selected fraction, raw child at that same fraction, and independently qualified raw child (or parent fallback). Test both execution modes, with matched noise. Charge every execution, including duplicate controllers. The final guided policy must improve complete mean cost by at least .001 over all three references, with capture nonregression in both modes and at least 90% deterministic capture. These final outcomes cannot select the fractions or modify training.

Freeze maxima of 21,600 actual scalar controls and actor rows: 8,800 qualification controls plus 12,800 final controls. Optimizer and model-query budgets are zero. If no fraction qualifies, close the run after the 8,800 qualification controls; unused final reservations cannot fund extra attempts. No retries, extra fractions or criterion changes. Inputs, actual actor versions, query ledgers and source are retained. Report setup, execution and audit wall time separately. Reused v3 and precision costs are shared paid ancestry, not free learning data.

Seed roles: qualification 151,000–151,015 with noise 153,000; final 152,000–152,063 with noise 154,000. All candidates are checkpointed before any qualification forward. Selection is recorded before final execution. This is a test of complete-outcome step acceptance, an established optimization component. A positive result still requires integration and at least two fresh feedback rounds before a bidirectional learning claim.
