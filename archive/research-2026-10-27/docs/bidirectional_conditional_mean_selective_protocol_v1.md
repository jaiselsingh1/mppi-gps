# Selective mean-controller assistance: prospective revision

This is an explicitly outcome-informed DEVELOPMENT revision of
`bidirectional_conditional_mean_protocol_v0.md`, not a repaired verdict for
conditional001 and not a frozen scientific comparison. Conditional001 failed
its75%-of-pairs teacher gate and stays failed. Its trial GUIDE improved five of
16 complete costs, tied11 and lost none. Those observations motivate a different
question: can assistance be selectively useful, with no observed paired cost
loss, and then improve the next standalone student? They cannot confirm it.

No control, reward, search, actor, memory, geometry, learning-work or budget setting
changes. The only experimental changes are the teacher pair criterion, fresh
root namespace and explicit disclosure. This is a qualification hypothesis
revision, not a new control algorithm. The previous physical and both-mode
negatives remain unchanged. Success still requires TWO beneficial feedback
rounds, matched RAW-data children and a separate untouched64-root final panel.

## New teacher criterion, fixed before any new call

At EVERY teacher comparison (initial trial qualification, actual promoted
confirmation, acquisition, changed actor/memory qualification and confirmation,
and final), require against EACH reference:

1. Mean complete actual cost gain>=.01.
2. Every paired complete cost gain>=-1e-9; this tolerance handles arithmetic
   equality, not a tunable harm allowance.
3. Aggregate capture nonregression and GUIDE capture>=90%.

References are strong RAW and the guiding actor alone; after each child update
they additionally include OLDGUIDE under the same updated memory. Report all
positive, tied and negative pairs and the fraction improving by>=.01. There is
no minimum improvement-prevalence claim. This exchanges the old broad-benefit
requirement for a stricter observed-loss restriction; it is not a guarantee of
statewise or population noninterference. Zero observed losses on finite panels
cannot establish that guarantee. Hypothetical min selection cannot replace the
actual full-controller comparisons.

Use genuinely fresh16-root trial qualification, then one promotion, then fresh16
actual promoted-controller confirmation. Do not reuse conditional001 roots or
costs as new confirmation. The new exact roots are801000--831063, with disjoint
stage panels as specified in the JSON; final831000--831063 is touched only after
both rounds pass. Config schema is
`bidirectional-ending-conditional-mean-feedback-v2-selective`, teacher rule
`zero_observed_paired_loss`. Unknown mixtures of old/new settings are rejected.
