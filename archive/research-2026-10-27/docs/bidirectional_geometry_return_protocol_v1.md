# State-informed policy geometry, ordinary complete-return learning

This is one new finite learner mechanism test, not a completed bidirectional GPS comparison. The closed coherent-return test learned a useful ending but ordinary-only, both short-data mixtures and one long-data mixture selected identical final policies. Saved outcomes contained genuine ending contrasts on long-search acquired states. A separate saved-array diagnosis found mostly bias-aligned random head responses on24 tested initial states, with nonzero residual directions. These observations motivate testing whether discovered states can improve the policy's update geometry without mixing hard acquired-state rewards into its deployment objective. They do not establish a capacity limit or a remedy.

Use the exact weak bootstrap, frozen128-unit trunk and unchanged25-control Ending task from audited early_context_v5. The teacher sources are the same eight complete short-search episodes and eight complete successful long-search episodes. Only pre-action observations and matching physical states at indices0–11 are read for geometry; the gate is at12. No teacher actions, teacher returns, future states, new action labels, critic or acquired-state rollout objective enter learning. Success/failure metadata and complete source traces remain bound for provenance. The source episodes and bootstrap training are paid ancestry, not free new discovery.

Save inputs, features, parent outputs and exact source-row references. This is a new inference measurement and is charged.

Construct B with four orthonormal rows from QR of a128×4 standard-normal matrix drawn by private PCG64 seed1701, with canonical QR signs. Four matched learner chains use: (1) fixed old .1B weight directions and zero bias offsets; (2) ordinary-state geometry; (3) ordinary plus short-source geometry; (4) ordinary plus long-source geometry. For each calibrated chain let A=HBᵀ, μ=mean(A) and A−μ=U diag(s) V, with V's rows the right singular vectors. Orient each row so its largest-magnitude entry is positive. Axis i changes the head weights by c_i BᵀV_i and head bias by −c_i μ·V_i, where c_i=min(.1√192/s_i,1,.5/|μ·V_i|). A zero bias denominator imposes no bias cap. Do not rescale or replace axes after seeing outcomes.

All chains have the same separate .1 bias axis and .5 log-standard-deviation axis. At each of three iterations, instantiate13 absolute-coordinate candidates around the current center: the center and ±one step on each of six axes. The absolute coordinate L1 norm is at most3 around the same parent. Keep the trunk, family identity0 and all other parameters fixed; retain all candidates, including duplicates. Iteration s uses ordinary roots622000+100s through622007+100s and private stochastic seed624000+100s, paired across candidates and chains.

A candidate must not increase mean complete cost or reduce aggregate capture count versus its freshly executed center in either mode. Choose the lowest objective with stable candidate index ties. Changed parameters always receive their own measured returns. This is critic-free whole-policy return search, not a policy-gradient or imitation result.

No retries, added directions, teacher-return mixing or within-run tuning are allowed.

A pass would establish a finite state-informed learner advantage in this small policy subspace. It would not establish full parameter optimization, policy-gradient superiority, independent physical Acrobot discovery, three-seed standalone qualification, two beneficial feedback rounds or total sample efficiency. A negative does not erase successful ending learning or prove all state-informed methods impossible. Independent MPPI proposals remain untouched in this learner diagnostic.
