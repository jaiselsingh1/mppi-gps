# Ending v3: exact actor continuation, protected independent search

Prospective development revision; all earlier agreement failures remain unchanged. This cheap scalar task is a mechanism test, not Acrobot/Walker success, a novel rollout primitive, or an efficiency result.

The saved v2 bank audit found good-lane candidates in all 256 gate-reaching guided banks. Of 104 bad selections, 87 also had a bad best augmented sample; 17 replaced a good best sample with a bad verified mean. Twenty of 31 failing episodes discarded an earlier successful forecast. An independently derived counterexample shows that even an exact successful actor candidate can lose under H4 task cost plus action agreement. The ending penalty is outside that objective. This motivates changing the continuation evaluated, rather than simply adding actor proposals to that score.

Use the identical initial discovery, actor and own-MC schedule as v1/v2: eight independent long-search teachers, 2,000 auxiliary updates, two 64-trajectory actor-owned return updates, retained provisional training, and separate fresh deterministic/stochastic deployment checks. No critic is trained. Auxiliary action supervision is explicit and is not the source of long-term credit. Actor-owned complete returns, including final feedback, drive the policy-gradient term.

At each supported actual state, the H4 independent MPPI bank, RNG, nominal and memory update first, without actor access. Extract no more than five first actions: the actor mean, independent champion, and empirical min/lower-median/max sampled first actions. Exact duplicate actions are removed. For each candidate simulate that one action followed by the frozen deterministic actor through the true ending. Rank by complete raw cost, with actor-first ties. Execute only the selected first action and repeat. There is no action-agreement penalty or synthesized guided mean. The private independent memory retains only its original independent discovery experience.

The target is exact model-simulated Q of the frozen actor's continuation. It is neither the measured return of future replanning nor a label for the updated actor. Under exact deterministic dynamics, full Markov state, complete actor tails and uninterrupted application, inclusion of the actor action gives c(s,a)+J_actor(next) <= J_actor(s), which telescopes to complete executed cost no worse than the actor. State-support fallback and numerical/model error limit this argument. Fresh executed comparisons remain mandatory. One-step rollout/policy improvement is established prior art; see [Bertsekas, Rollout and Policy Iteration](https://stuff.mit.edu/~dimitrib/Multiagent_Sinica_2020.pdf). The research object is the qualified bidirectional learning loop.

Each actor version begins with zero influence. Sixteen complete suffix tests at declared support anchors may permit trial use. Two disjoint 16-root paired whole-controller panels qualify then confirm the new selector. Both panels require capture nonregression and mean complete cost improvement >= .01 against independent search and the actor, with >=75% paired improvements >= .01, >=90% guided capture and changed executed actions. The confirmation inherits only the original suffix evidence. Every actual controller hash binds selector protocol, actor and trust evidence. Model query failures close the run; invalidated actor/support returns influence to zero.

After confirmation, acquire eight fresh paired independent/guided teacher episodes and evaluate the actor on those roots. The same strict teacher gate applies. Evaluate fresh executed independent/guided/actor controllers on 64 disjoint roots. Retain failures. Only actual successful teacher traces supply auxiliary actions.

Collect 64 fresh complete stochastic trajectories from the unchanged parent: eight ordinary and eight acquired contexts, four replicates each. Clone the complete parent/optimizer/RNG twice. Use identical owned-return data and optimizer work; each child gets the same fixed 100 common successful teacher rows plus 100 successful new teacher rows from its arm. If that arm has no successful new teacher, its second stratum explicitly reuses the shared corpus. Fixed evenly spaced indices resample short strata; both children retain identical common rows and equal work. Parent, raw-data child and guided-data child receive paired, fresh 64-root deterministic evaluations and separate stochastic evaluations with matched noise. The guided child must improve complete mean cost by >= .001 over both parent and raw-data child with capture nonregression in both modes, and >=90% deterministic capture. Low action-fit loss alone cannot pass. Exact revocation must restore independent actions and complete cost. These per-round child panels are fresh promotion/validation data. Repeat this whole feedback sequence once with the accepted guided child and fresh roots. Its prior trust expires. After both rounds, a separate end-only 64-root deterministic actor panel compares the accepted child, its raw-data sibling and its immediate parent. It is used for no training or parent selection and must pass the same child cost/capture criteria.

The checked-in configuration declares a maximum of 2,370,686 scalar controls, 841,866 actor inference rows, 2,168 Adam calls and six diagnostic backward traversals, including initial training and two rounds. Unused reservations are not expenditure. Every model/live control, failure, neural forward, optimizer and gradient call is metered before execution. All candidate tails and actual episodes are retained; analysis time and arithmetic audit work are reported separately. Each gate failure closes this version without retries or tuning. The overall development project continues through separately specified revisions.

Seed roles per round (round 1 adds 10,000): qualification 61,000–61,015; confirmation 62,000–62,015; acquisition 63,000–63,007; own ordinary 64,000–64,007; held control 65,000–65,063; held children 66,000–66,063; revocation 67,000; stochastic noise 68,000. The end-only pipeline holdout uses 90,000–90,063. Acquired contexts use only retained discovery states. Initial discovery/training roots retain the v1 schedule. No held outcome is selected as teacher data. Results are finite development evidence; three paired training seeds and the original physical-task criteria remain outstanding.
